Initial release of `@ez-gform/cli`: an `ez-gform` `npx` tool that fetches a public Google Form and emits JSON/`types`/`react`/`html` via `@ez-gform/codegen`, supports offline `--json-input` for saved `FB_PUBLIC_LOAD_DATA_`/HTML, and a `submit` subcommand for smoke-testing a payload against the live `formResponse` endpoint.
